SocietyBites Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

SocietyBites ("SocietyBites", "we", "our", or "us") is a community-based homemade food marketplace that helps residents discover, buy, and share homemade food within their community.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the SocietyBites mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use SocietyBites, we may collect the following types of information:

Account Information: We may collect your mobile phone number and information required to create and authenticate your account.

Profile Information: You may provide information such as your name, society or community, flat or unit information, and other profile details.

Food Listings: If you use SocietyBites as a seller, we may collect information about the food items you list, including item names, descriptions, prices, availability, images, and related listing information.

Order Information: When you place or receive an order, we may collect information necessary to process and manage the order, including order details, quantities, prices, order status, and pickup information.

Payment Information: Sellers may provide payment-related details, such as UPI information, for receiving payments. We do not collect or store your bank account password, UPI PIN, or other payment authentication credentials.

Usage and Technical Information: We may collect limited technical information required to operate, secure, maintain, and improve the application, such as device information, application activity, diagnostic information, and error information.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

• Create and manage user accounts.
• Authenticate users and maintain account security.
• Display food listings and seller information.
• Enable users to place and manage orders.
• Allow sellers to create and manage food listings.
• Process and track orders and pickups.
• Display relevant information within the user's community.
• Provide customer support and respond to requests.
• Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.
• Maintain and improve SocietyBites and its features.

3. Information Shared With Other Users

SocietyBites is designed as a community marketplace. Some information may therefore be visible to other users when necessary to provide the service.

For example, seller information associated with a food listing and information necessary to coordinate an order or pickup may be displayed to relevant users.

We do not make your private account credentials publicly available.

4. Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party service providers to operate and support SocietyBites, including services for authentication, cloud hosting, data storage, analytics, security, and application infrastructure.

These service providers may process information on our behalf only as necessary to provide their services and operate the application.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide SocietyBites services, maintain accounts and transaction records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and protect the security and integrity of the service.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information associated with your SocietyBites account, subject to applicable laws and legitimate business or legal requirements.

You may also stop using the application at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

SocietyBites is intended for general audiences and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

9. Third-Party Links and Services

SocietyBites may use or interact with third-party services. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services when applicable.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to SocietyBites, our practices, or applicable requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us through the SocietyBites support page.
