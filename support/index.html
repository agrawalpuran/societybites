SocietyBites Support

Welcome to SocietyBites support.

SocietyBites is a community-based homemade food marketplace that helps residents discover, buy and sell homemade food within their community.

Need Help?

Common Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the available food listings, select an item and follow the ordering process in the app.

How do I sell food?

Eligible residents can create food listings through the seller functionality in the SocietyBites app.

How can I manage my orders?

Your orders can be viewed and managed from the Orders section of the app.
