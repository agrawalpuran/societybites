# SocietyBites

## Overview

SocietyBites is a hyperlocal food marketplace that enables residents of a gated community to buy homemade food from other residents within the same society.

The platform focuses on trust, convenience and community by allowing buyers to order food prepared by verified home chefs in their own apartment complex.

The initial launch is targeted at one society and is designed to scale to multiple societies in the future.

---

## Vision

Build the easiest and most trusted platform for buying homemade food within residential communities.

---

## Target Users

### Buyer

Residents who want fresh homemade food.

### Seller

Residents who prepare and sell homemade food.

### Super Admin

Platform owner responsible for managing societies and the application.

---

## Core Features

- Firebase OTP Login
- Buyer & Seller Roles
- Food Listings
- Cart
- Checkout
- Direct UPI Payment
- Orders
- Reviews
- Seller Dashboard
- Multi-Society Support
- Super Admin Portal

---

## Tech Stack

Frontend: Flutter

Backend: Node.js + Express

Database: Supabase PostgreSQL

ORM: Prisma

Authentication: Firebase OTP

Storage: Supabase Storage

Version Control: GitHub

AI Development: Cursor
